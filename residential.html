Why Tint Your Home

THE BENEFITS

Window film is one of the most cost-effective upgrades for any Long Island home — reducing energy bills, protecting furniture, and adding privacy year-round.

☀️
UV Protection

Block up to 99% of harmful UV rays that fade furniture, flooring, and artwork over time. Protect your investment without changing your view.

🌡️
Heat Reduction

Reduce solar heat gain by up to 80%, keeping rooms cooler in summer and lowering your air conditioning costs — especially in south-facing rooms.

🔒
Privacy & Security

Daytime privacy film lets you see out while blocking the view in. Security film holds shattered glass together, reducing injury risk from breakage.

💡
Glare Reduction

Eliminate screen glare on TVs and monitors without losing natural light. Work and watch in comfort without closing blinds all day.

🏠
Curb Appeal

Exterior-facing film gives your home a clean, uniform look that enhances curb appeal while protecting your interior from the elements.

⚡
Energy Savings

Reduce your energy bill year-round. Window film pays for itself — keeping heat out in summer and retaining warmth in winter.

Film Options

CHOOSE YOUR FILM

Every home is different. We'll help you select the right film for your windows, light preference, and privacy needs.

Most Popular
Solar Control Film

The go-to choice for heat and UV rejection while maintaining a clear view. Available in multiple tint levels to control how much light comes in.

  • Rejects up to 80% solar heat
  • Blocks up to 99% UV rays
  • Multiple shade options
  • Maintains natural light
Privacy
Privacy Film

See-through from inside, opaque from outside during the day. Perfect for street-facing windows, bathrooms, and ground-floor rooms.

  • One-way daytime privacy
  • Frosted options available
  • Natural light preserved
  • No curtains needed
Safety
Security Film

Holds glass together on impact — reducing injury risk from accidents, break-ins, or severe weather. Ideal for sliding doors and large windows.

  • Shatter-resistant
  • Break-in deterrent
  • Storm protection
  • Near-invisible finish
