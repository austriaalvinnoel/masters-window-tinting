What is Paint Protection Film (PPF)?

PPF is a transparent protective film installed over painted vehicle surfaces to help reduce damage from rock chips, road debris, light scratches and other everyday driving hazards.

Which parts of my car should I protect?

Many drivers start with the bumper, hood, fenders and mirrors because these areas see the most road impact. Full-vehicle coverage is also available for owners who want broader protection.

What is the difference between PPF and Liquid PPF?

Traditional PPF is a physical film applied to the vehicle. Liquid PPF is a seamless liquid-applied protection system. Masters offers both, so we can help you compare the two approaches.

Can I combine PPF with ceramic coating?

Paint protection film and ceramic coating serve different purposes and can be part of the same overall paint-protection plan. Contact us so we can recommend the right combination for your vehicle.
