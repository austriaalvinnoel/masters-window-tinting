PPF Questions

Frequently Asked

Is color change PPF the same as Liquid PPF?

No. Color change PPF is a physical film that changes the vehicle’s appearance while adding protection. Liquid PPF is a separate liquid-applied protection product.

Can I get clear Paint Protection Film?

Yes. Clear PPF is the traditional choice for customers who want physical film protection while keeping the factory color visible.

Do I need full-body PPF?

Not necessarily. Coverage should be based on your vehicle, driving habits, budget, and protection goals.

What if I am deciding between PPF and Liquid PPF?

We can explain both. Traditional PPF is a physical film system, while Liquid PPF is a separate protection option. You can also review the dedicated Liquid PPF page.
