Our Story

BUILT ON
CRAFTSMANSHIP

Masters Window Tinting was founded in West Babylon with one goal: deliver professional-grade window tinting and paint protection that lasts — backed by real expertise, not just marketing.

For 25+ years, we have grown into one of Long Island's most trusted auto protection shops, serving customers across Nassau County, Suffolk County, and beyond. We have never compromised on quality — every job is done by our own trained, full-time installers.

When we became a Certified Ceramic Pro installer, it wasn't just a credential — it validated the standard we had always held ourselves to. We're proud to be one of a small number of shops on Long Island with that designation.

Our History

THE JOURNEY

2000s

Founded in West Babylon

Masters Window Tinting opens its doors at 863 Rte 109, West Babylon — offering professional automotive window tinting to Nassau and Suffolk County drivers.

2009

Expanded to Residential & Commercial

Due to growing demand, we expanded our services beyond automotive to include home, office, and commercial building window film installations across Long Island.

2014

Launched Paint Protection Film Services

Added full paint protection film (PPF) installation, giving Long Island car owners access to professional-grade paint protection previously only available at high-end dealerships.

2018

Became a Certified Ceramic Pro Installer

Achieved official Ceramic Pro certification — one of only a few shops on Long Island to earn this designation — adding ceramic coating to our lineup of professional services.

2022

Introduced NanoPro Liquid PPF Technology

Launched our signature NanoPro Liquid PPF formula — an all-in-one ceramic coating and paint protection film with no seams, no bubbles, and a fraction of the cost of traditional PPF.

Today

Still Going Strong on Long Island

Over 25 years in, we're still at the same location, still using the same full-time team, and still backing every job with the same lifetime warranty standards we built our reputation on.
