Common Questions

LIQUID PPF FAQ

What's the difference between Liquid PPF and traditional PPF?

+
Traditional PPF is a thick plastic film cut to size and applied in sheets — it can leave seams and bubbles at panel edges. Our NanoPro Liquid PPF is a spray-on formula that leaves no seams, no visible edges, and full coverage for a fraction of the cost.

Is Liquid PPF really self-healing?

+
Yes. Light swirl marks and minor surface scratches in the protective layer heal with heat — from the sun or warm water — returning the surface to smooth. This keeps your paint looking flawless over time.

How long does it last?

+
Durability depends on coverage level and care, but Liquid PPF is engineered for long-term protection. We'll walk you through the expected lifespan for your chosen package and how simple maintenance keeps it performing.

Can it go over the whole car?

+
Absolutely. We offer everything from partial front protection for high-impact zones to full-body coverage for complete, head-to-toe protection. We tailor the coverage to your vehicle, your driving, and your budget.

Can I combine Liquid PPF with ceramic coating?

+
Yes — many customers pair them. Liquid PPF provides the impact and self-healing protection, while a ceramic coating on top adds extra gloss and hydrophobic properties. Ask us about combined packages when you call.
