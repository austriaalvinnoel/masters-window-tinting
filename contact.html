Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

+
Walk-ins are welcome for estimates and quick jobs. For full tinting, ceramic coating, or PPF installs, we recommend calling ahead to schedule a time so we can have the bay ready for your vehicle.

How long does window tinting take?

+
A typical automotive window tint takes 2–4 hours depending on the vehicle. Larger vehicles or multiple services may take longer. We'll give you an estimated time when you schedule.

What's the difference between Liquid PPF and traditional PPF?

+
Traditional PPF is a thick plastic film cut to size and applied in sheets — it can leave seams and bubbles at panel edges. Our NanoPro Liquid PPF is a spray-on formula that leaves no seams, no visible edges, and full coverage for a fraction of the cost.

Is there a warranty on your work?

+
Yes — lifetime warranty covers bubbling, peeling, and fading with full replacement of failed material and workmanship. We stand behind every job we do.

Do you serve areas outside West Babylon?

+
Our shop is in West Babylon and we serve customers from across Nassau and Suffolk County — including Babylon, Amityville, Copiague, Lindenhurst, Deer Park, Bay Shore, and all of Long Island. Most customers find it's a short drive worth making.

Do you offer discounts?

+
Yes — we offer discounts for military, police, and first responders. Mention it when you call or submit the form.
