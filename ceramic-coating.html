Common Questions

CERAMIC FAQ

How long does a ceramic coating last?

+
It depends on the package. A single-layer coating typically lasts about a year, while multi-layer Ceramic Pro and our BOROGRAPH 2.0 graphene coating are engineered for multi-year durability. Proper maintenance washing extends the life significantly.

Is ceramic coating better than wax?

+
Yes — by a wide margin. Wax sits on top of the paint and washes away in weeks. A ceramic coating chemically bonds to your clear coat, creating a hard, durable layer that lasts years and provides far stronger gloss, hydrophobic, and chemical-resistance properties.

Do I need paint correction first?

+
A coating locks in whatever condition the paint is in, so any swirls or scratches will be sealed under it. For the best result we recommend paint correction beforehand — our detailing team handles this in-house so the coating goes over flawless paint.

What is BOROGRAPH 2.0?

+
BOROGRAPH 2.0 is our flagship Elite NanoPro coating infused with boron silicate and graphene-oxide technology. It produces the deepest gloss and the hardest, most durable surface we offer — reserved for our certified Elite NanoPro installers.

How do I maintain a coated vehicle?

+
Maintenance is easy — that's the point. A regular hand wash with a pH-neutral soap keeps the coating performing. Avoid automatic brush car washes. We'll give you simple aftercare guidance when you pick up your vehicle.
