Window Tinting

Best Window Tint for Long Island Heat & Sun

Masters Window Tinting · West Babylon, NY · 5 min read

Why Long Island Heat Is Harder on Your Car Than You Think

Long Island summers push interior temperatures to 140°F or higher on a parked car. That's not just uncomfortable — it accelerates dashboard cracking, leather fading, and electronic component failure. The right window film can cut that by 40–60%.

The 3 Types of Film We Install

Not all tint is created equal. Here's what we offer at Masters and who each film is best for:

  • Dyed Film — Entry-level, affordable, decent privacy. Does not block heat as effectively. Not recommended for Long Island summers.
  • Carbon Film — Our most popular choice. Blocks up to 40% of heat, won't fade, and provides a clean matte look. Great value for everyday vehicles.
  • Ceramic Film — Top-tier heat rejection (up to 60%), crystal clarity, and zero signal interference (won't affect GPS, phones, or toll transponders). Best for luxury vehicles and anyone who spends a lot of time in their car.

📍 Long Island specific tip: Salt air from the ocean accelerates adhesive degradation in cheap film. We only install quality film with UV-stable adhesives that hold up in the coastal environment.

What Shade Should You Get?

New York State law requires front side windows to allow at least 70% of light through. Rear windows and the rear windshield can be any shade. Most of our customers choose:

  • 35% on rear windows — Balanced look, good privacy, significant heat reduction
  • 20% on rear windows — Very dark, maximum privacy and heat block
  • 70% (legal limit) on front windows — Nearly invisible, but still blocks UV and reduces glare

How Long Does It Last?

All film we install comes with a lifetime warranty on film and labor — no bubbling, no peeling, no fading. If it fails for any reason, we replace it free.
