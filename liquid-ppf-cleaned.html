Is Liquid PPF as strong as traditional film?

For most driving conditions, yes. Liquid PPF handles daily driving, rock chips, and UV perfectly. For extreme off-road, traditional film on the leading edges adds more physical thickness.

How long does Liquid PPF last?

NanoPro Liquid PPF lasts 2–5 years depending on the package and maintenance. Our premium packages include a full replacement warranty.

Can it be applied over existing paint damage?

Liquid PPF bonds to the paint surface — it should be applied to clean, corrected paint. We can perform paint correction before application if needed.

Can I combine it with ceramic coating?

Yes — and we highly recommend it. Liquid PPF for physical chip protection, ceramic coating on top for gloss, hydrophobic protection, and ease of cleaning.

How long until I can wash my car?

We recommend waiting 7 days before washing. Avoid high-pressure water on edges for the first 2 weeks to allow full cure.
