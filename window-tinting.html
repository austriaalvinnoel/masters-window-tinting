How long does window tinting take?

Most vehicles take 2–4 hours. Larger vehicles (SUVs, trucks) may take up to 6 hours. Same-day service is available on most jobs.

How long before I can roll down my windows?

We recommend waiting 3–5 days before rolling down windows to allow the film to fully cure. In colder weather, allow up to 7 days.

Will tint affect my GPS or phone signal?

Our carbon and ceramic films do not interfere with GPS, cell signals, or toll transponders. Only metalized film causes interference — we don't use it.

What does the lifetime warranty cover?

Our lifetime warranty covers bubbling, peeling, fading, and delamination for the life of the vehicle. Full replacement at no charge if anything fails.

Do you tint the front windshield?

NY law prohibits tinting the front windshield below the AS-1 line. We can apply a clear UV film to the full windshield that blocks UV without affecting visibility.
